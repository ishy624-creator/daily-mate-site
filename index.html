Daily Mate

A new checkmate puzzle every day.

Daily Mate is a YouTube channel that posts short chess puzzles. Each video shows a position from a real game, gives you a few seconds to find the checkmate, then plays the solution.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@DailyMateChess

Where the puzzles come from

Every puzzle is taken from the Lichess open puzzle database, which is released into the public domain (CC0). Each video description links to the original puzzle and game.

How the videos are made

The channel uses a small personal tool, Daily Mate Uploader, that draws each puzzle as a video and schedules it on the Daily Mate channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel owner and only uploads to the channel owner's own account.
